Returns & Refunds

We want you to feel confident with every purchase. If something isn't right, our return process is simple and transparent.

package_2 keyboard_return

Returns & Eligibility

You may return a product if:

  • check_circle The item is unused and in original condition.
  • check_circle All original packaging and accessories are included.
  • check_circle The return request is made within 7 days of delivery.

How to Request a Return

1.

Contact our support team with your order number.

2.

Share the reason for your return request.

3.

Our team will guide you through the next steps.

payments

Refund Process

1.

Approved returns are inspected upon arrival at our facility.

2.

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of approval.

3.

Amount is credited back to the original payment method used.

content_paste_search

Damaged or Incorrect Items

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with clear images for quick assistance. We prioritize these cases for immediate replacement.

Non-Returnable Items

Items that have been assembled, items purchased in clearance, or customized items are not eligible for return.
